    2.

    Evaluate the Laplace transforms of the following functions:
    🔗

    (a)

    \(\displaystyle{f(t) = u(t - \pi)\cos(3(t - \pi))}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\L\{ u(t - \pi) \cos(3(t -\pi))\} = \frac{e^{-\pi s}s}{s^2 + 3^2} \)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    \(\displaystyle{f(t) = \sin(5t) + 4\delta(t - 1) + 2t^3}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\L\{ \sin(5t) + 4\delta(t - 1) + 2t^3\} = \frac{5}{s^2 + 5^2} + 4e^{-s} + 2 \cdot \frac{3!}{s^{3 + 1}}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    \(\displaystyle{f(t) = e^{-2t} t^5 + 3}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\L\{ e^{-2t} t^5 + 3\} = \frac{5!}{(s + 2)^{5 + 1}} + \frac{3}{s} \)

    3.

    Solve the differential equation: \(\displaystyle{y'' + 9y = 4 \sec(3t)}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The associated homogeneous equation has characteristic equation \(r^2 + 9 = 0\text{,}\) so the general solution is:
    \begin{gather*} y_c = C_1 \underbrace{\sin(3t)}_{y_1} + C_2 \underbrace{\cos(3t)}_{y_2} \end{gather*}
    The Wronskian is:
    \begin{align*} W \amp = \begin{vmatrix} \sin(3t) \amp \cos(3t) \\ 3\cos(3t) \amp -3\sin(3t) \end{vmatrix} \\ \amp = -3\sin^2(3t) - 3\cos^2(3t) \\ \amp = -3 \end{align*}
    Now:
    \begin{align*} u_1' \amp = \frac{-\cos(3t) 4\sec(3t)}{-3} = \frac{4}{3} \amp u_1 \amp = \frac{4t}{3} \\ u_2' \amp = \frac{\sin(3t) 4\sec(3t)}{-3} = \frac{-4}{3} \tan(3t) \amp u_2 \amp = \frac{-4}{9} \ln|\sec(3t)| \end{align*}
    Finally, the general solution is:
    \begin{align*} y \amp = y_c + y_p \\ \amp = C_1 \sin(3t) + C_2 \cos(3t) + \frac{4t \sin(3t)}{3} - \frac{4\ln|\sec(3t)|}{9} \cos(3t) \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    4.

    Evaluate \(\displaystyle{\mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{\frac{1}{s^2(s + 1)}\right\}}\) using a convolution integral.
    🔗
    (Work involving partial fractions decomposition will not receive credit for this problem.)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{align*} \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{\frac{1}{s^2(s + 1)}\right\} \amp = \L \left\{\frac{1}{s^2}\right\} \ast \L \left\{\frac{1}{s + 1}\right\} \\ \amp = t \ast e^{-t} \\ \amp = \int_0^t (t - \tau)e^{-\tau}\ d\tau \amp u = t - \tau, v = -e^{-\tau} \\ \amp = -(t - \tau)e^{-\tau}\bigg|_0^t - \int_0^t e^{-\tau}\ d\tau \amp du = -d\tau, dv = e^{-\tau}\ d\tau \\ \amp = 0 - t - (-e^{-\tau})\bigg|_0^t\\ \amp = 0 + t + e^{-t} - 1. \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    5.

    Solve \(\displaystyle{y'' + 4y = 5 \delta(t - 3)}\text{,}\) \(y(0) = 1\text{,}\) \(y'(0) = 0\) using the Laplace transform.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{align*} s^2Y - sy(0) - y'(0) + 4Y \amp = 5e^{-3s} \\ (s^2 + 4)Y - s \amp = 5e^{-3s} \\ Y \amp = \frac{s}{s^2 + 4} + \frac{5e^{-3s}}{s^2 + 4} \\ y(t) \amp = \cos(2t) + \frac{5}{2}u(t - 3)\sin(2(t - 3)) \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    6.

    Find the inverse Laplace transform: \(\displaystyle{\mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{\frac{8e^{-4s}}{s^2 + 2s - 3}\right\}}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    First, PFD:
    \begin{align*} \frac{8}{(s + 3)(s - 1)} = \frac{A}{s + 3} + \frac{B}{s - 1} \\ 8 \amp = A(s - 1) + B(s + 3) \\ s = 1: \qquad 8 \amp = 4B \quad \Rightarrow \quad B = 2 \amp \\ s = -3: \qquad 8 \amp = -4A \quad \Rightarrow \quad A = -2 \amp \end{align*}
    So:
    \begin{align*} \L^{-1}\left\{ \frac{8e^{-4s}}{(s + 3)(s - 1)}\right\} \amp = \L^{-1}\left\{ \frac{-2e^{-4s}}{s + 3} + \frac{2e^{-4s}}{s - 1}\right\} \\ \amp = -2 u(t - 4) e^{-3(t - 4)} + 2 u(t - 4) e^{t - 4} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    7.

    Calculate the determinants of the following matrices, and find the inverse of \(A\text{.}\) (You do not need to find the inverse of \(B\text{.}\))
    \begin{align*} A = \begin{bmatrix} 3 \amp 1 \\ -1 \amp -4 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp 1 \amp 3 \\ 1 \amp 3 \amp 4 \\ -2 \amp 1 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{align*}
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{align*} \begin{vmatrix} 3 \amp 1 \\ -1 \amp -4 \end{vmatrix} \amp = (3)(-4) - (1)(-1) = -11 \\ A^{-1} \amp = \frac{1}{-11} \begin{bmatrix} -4 \amp -1 \\ 1 \amp 3 \end{bmatrix} \\ \begin{vmatrix} 2 \amp 1 \amp 3 \\ 1 \amp 3 \amp 4 \\ -2 \amp 1 \amp 1 \end{vmatrix} \amp = (2)(3)(1) + (1)(4)(-2) + (3)(1)(1)\\ \amp \quad -(2)(4)(1) - (1)(1)(1) - (3)(3)(-2) \\ \amp = 6 - 8 + 3 - 8 - 1 + 18 \\ \amp = 10. \end{align*}
