    Subsection Mixing

    Suppose a tank contains a solution of some substance dissolved in liquid. There an in-pipe bringing a solution into the tank and an out-pipe draining solution from the tank. We can model the quantity \(Q(t)\) of substance in the tank at time \(t\) with the differential equation setup:
    \begin{gather*} \frac{dQ}{dt} = \left( \text{rate of substance in} \right) - \left( \text{rate of substance out} \right) \end{gather*}
    🔗

    Example 16.

    A tank starts with 200 L of fresh water. Brine with 1.5 g/L salt flows in at 10 L/min. The mixture flows out of the tank at the same rate. Find \(Q(t)\text{,}\) the amount of salt in the tank at time \(t\text{.}\) Then:
    \begin{align*} \frac{dQ}{dt} \amp = \left(1.5 \frac{\text{g}}{\text{L}}\right)\left( 10 \frac{\text{L}}{\text{min}}\right) - \left(\frac{Q}{200} \frac{\text{g}}{\text{L}}\right) - \left(10 \frac{\text{L}}{\text{min}}\right) \\ \frac{dQ}{dt} \amp = 15 - \frac{Q}{20} \end{align*}
    This is a linear equation so we can solve it with an integrating factor:
    \begin{align*} Q' + \frac{1}{20} Q \amp = 15 \\ r(t) \amp = e^{\int \frac{1}{20}\ dt} = e^{t/20} \\ \left(e^{t/20} Q\right)' \amp = 15e^{t/20} \\ e^{t/20} Q \amp = 300e^{t/20} + C \\ Q \amp = 300 + Ce^{-t/20} \end{align*}
    Since the tank starts with fresh water, we have the initial condition \(Q(0) = 0\text{:}\)
    \begin{align*} 0 \amp = 300 + Ce^0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad C = -300 \\ Q(t) \amp = 300 - 300 e^{-t/20} \end{align*}
    Observe that \(Q(t) \to 300\) as \(t \to \infty\text{,}\) and 300 g in a 200 L tank would be a concentration of 1.5 g/L, which is reasonable.

    Subsection Autonomous Equations

    Definition 17.

    An autonomous equation has the form \(y' = f(y)\text{.}\) A solution of the form \(y(x) = k\) is called an equilibrium.
    🔗
    🔗

    Example 18.

    Consider \(Q' = 15 - \frac{Q}{20}\text{.}\) To find an equlibrium solution, set \(Q' = 0\text{:}\)
    \begin{align*} 0 \amp = 15 - \frac{Q}{20} \quad \Rightarrow \quad Q = 300. \end{align*}
    Note that, for any \(C\text{:}\)
    \begin{gather*} \lim_{t\to\infty} 300 + Ce^{-t/20} = 300. \end{gather*}
    While this is not entirely a coincidence, note that this limit property is not part of the definition of an equilibrium.
    🔗
