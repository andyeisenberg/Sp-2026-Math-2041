    1.

    For each function \(f(t)\) below, find the Laplace transform \(\mathcal{L}\{f(t)\} = F(s)\text{.}\)
    🔗

    (a)

    \(\displaystyle{f(t) = 2t^3 + 5e^{-t} + 8}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    \begin{align*} F(s) \amp = 2 \cdot \frac{3!}{s^{3 + 1}} + 5 \cdot \frac{1}{s - (-1)} + 8 \cdot \frac{1}{s} \\ \amp = \frac{12}{s^{4}} + \frac{5}{s + 1} + \frac{8}{s} \end{align*}

    2.

    Use variation of parameters to find the general solution of \(y'' - y = e^{x} + 4\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The associated homogenous equation is:
    \begin{gather*} y'' - y = 0, \end{gather*}
    which has characteristic equation \(f^2 - 1 = (r - 1)(r + 1) = 0\text{,}\) so \(r = \pm 1\text{.}\) Therefore, the general solution is:
    \begin{gather*} y_c = C_1 e^x + C_2 e^{-x}. \end{gather*}
    Let \(y_1 = e^x, y_2 = e^{-x}\text{.}\) The Wronskian is:
    \begin{align*} W \amp = \begin{vmatrix} e^x \amp e^{-x} \\ e^x \amp -e^{-x} \end{vmatrix} \\ \amp = (e^x)(-e^{-x}) - (e^{-x})(e^x) \\ \amp = -1 - 1 = -2. \end{align*}
    Now:
    \begin{align*} u_1' \amp = \frac{-e^{-x}(e^x + 4)}{-2} = \frac{1 + 4e^{-x}}{2} \quad \Rightarrow \quad u_1 = \frac{x}{2} - 2e^{-x} \\ u_2' \amp = \frac{e^x(e^x + 4)}{-2} = \frac{e^{2x} + 4e^{x}}{-2} \quad \Rightarrow \quad u_2 = \frac{-e^{2x}}{4} - 2e^x \end{align*}
    Now, the general solution to the nonhomogeneous equation is:
    \begin{align*} y \amp = C_1e^x + C_2e^{-x} + \left(\frac{x}{2} - 2e^{-x}\right)e^x + \left(\frac{-e^{2x}}{4} - 2e^x\right)e^{-x} \\ \amp = C_1e^x + C_2e^{-x} + \frac{xe^x}{2} - 2 - \frac{e^x}{4} - 2 \\ \amp = C_1e^x + C_2e^{-x} + \frac{xe^x}{2} - 4. \end{align*}
