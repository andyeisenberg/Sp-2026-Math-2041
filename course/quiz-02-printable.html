    2.

    A tank initially contains 100 L of a dye solution with a concentration of 1 g/L. Clean water flows into the tank at a rate of 4 L/min, and the mixture in the tank flows out through a drain pipe at the same rate. Let \(Q(t)\) be the quantity of dye in the tank at time \(t\)
    🔗
    Write a differential equation involving \(Q(t)\) and \(Q'(t)\text{,}\) then solve your differential equation.
    🔗
    Solution.
    \(\frac{dQ}{dt} = \text{rate in} - \text{rate out}\text{.}\) Since clean water is flowing in, the rate in is 0. Therefore:
    \begin{align*} \frac{dQ}{dt} \amp = - (4)\left(\frac{Q}{100}\right) = \frac{-Q}{25} \\ \Rightarrow \quad \int \frac{dQ}{Q} \amp = \int \frac{-dt}{25} \\ \ln(Q) \amp = \frac{-t}{25} + C, \end{align*}
    so \(Q = Ce^{-t/25}\text{.}\) With an initial concentration of 1 g/L and 100 L of solution, we have \(Q(0) = 100 = Ce^0\text{,}\) so \(C = 100\text{.}\) Therefore the solution is
    \begin{gather*} Q(t) = 100e^{-t/25} \end{gather*}
    🔗

    3.

    Determine whether the equation \(\displaystyle{\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{2xe^y - y + 6x^2}{x - x^2e^y + 3}}\) is exact, then find an (implicit) solution.
    🔗
    Solution.
    In standard form:
    \begin{gather*} (y - 6x^2 - 2xe^y) + (x - x^2e^y + 3) \frac{dy}{dx} = 0. \end{gather*}
    Let \(M(x, y) = y - 6x^2 - 2xe^y\) and \(N(x, y) = x - x^2e^y + 3\text{.}\) Then \(M_y = 1 - 2xe^y = N_x\text{,}\) so the equation is exact. If \(F_x = M\text{,}\) then:
    \begin{align*} F(x, y) \amp = \int y - 6x^2 - 2xe^y\ dx = xy - 2x^3 - x^2e^y + g(y) \\ \Rightarrow \quad F_y \amp = x - x^2e^y + g'(y) = x - x^2e^y + 3 \\ \Rightarrow \quad g'(y) \amp = 3 \quad \Rightarrow \quad g(y) = 3y \end{align*}
    So \(F(x, y) = xy - 2x^3 - x^2e^y + 3y\text{,}\) and the implicit solution to the equation is:
    \begin{gather*} xy - 2x^3 - x^2e^y + 3y = C \end{gather*}
